Tambola number calls: all 90

The traditional nicknames a caller uses for every number, from 1 to 90.

Part of the fun of Tambola (Housie) is the caller's patter. Instead of just "twenty-two", a good caller says "Two little ducks, twenty-two". Many calls rhyme with the number, and others come from its shape or a bit of history. They make numbers easy to hear across a noisy room, and they make the game more fun.

Hosting your own game? Use this list, or let Housey's free number caller read them out for you: choose Traditional under "What the caller says". It can also call plain numbers ("Number 22") or digits ("Two and two, 22"). Need tickets? Print them here.

Some favourites explained

  • 11, Legs eleven: the two ones look like a pair of legs.
  • 22, Two little ducks: each 2 looks like a duck on the water.
  • 88, Two fat ladies: each 8 looks like a round figure.
  • 45, Halfway there: 45 is half of 90, the highest number.
  • 90, Top of the shop: the last and largest number in the bag.
