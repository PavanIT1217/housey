How to play
  • Numbers 1–90 are pulled from the bag one at a time and called out.
  • If the number is on your ticket, tap it to dab it with ink.
  • Early Five: first 5 numbers dabbed on a ticket. Top / Middle / Bottom Line: every number in that row. Full House: all 15 numbers.
  • Press the prize button to claim it. Claim a prize you haven't got and the caller shouts “Bogey!”.
  • The game ends when someone wins Full House.
