Housey is a free online Tambola game, also called Housie. Wooden number coins come out of a cloth bag, the caller reads out each number (plainly, or with traditional calls like "Two little ducks, twenty-two"), and you dab your paper ticket with ink. Play against friendly computer players and race them to every prize.

How to play Housey

  1. Choose 1 to 3 tickets, how many players sit at the table and how fast the caller goes.
  2. Numbers 1 to 90 are drawn one at a time. If a called number is on your ticket, tap it to dab it.
  3. Finish a prize and press its button to claim it before anyone else. A wrong claim is a "Bogey!".
  4. The game ends when someone wins Full House.

Frequently asked questions

Is Housey free to play?

Yes. Housey is free, with no ads, no sign-up and no in-app purchases. Prizes are points only and no real money is involved.

Is Housey the same as Tambola and Housie?

Yes. Housey, Housie and Tambola are names for the same 90-number game, played on tickets of 3 rows and 9 columns with 15 numbers each.

Does Housey work offline?

Yes. After your first visit the game saves itself on your device and works with no internet connection. You can also install it on your home screen like an app.

Which prizes can I win?

Early Five (the first five numbers on a ticket), Top Line, Middle Line, Bottom Line and Full House (all 15 numbers). The game ends when someone wins Full House.

Can I play Housey on my phone?

Yes. Housey works in any modern browser on phones, tablets and computers, and there are apps for Android and iPhone.

Can I print Tambola tickets?

Yes. The Print tickets page makes as many unique tickets as you need, in your colours, with a code and QR code on each. Print them or save a PDF.

Is there a number caller for games on paper tickets?

Yes. The Number caller draws numbers 1 to 90, reads them out loud at the speed you choose, and can check any Housey ticket by its code or QR code.

Will I lose my game if I refresh the page?

No. Housey saves your game as you play. After a refresh, choose Resume game to carry on.
