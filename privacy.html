Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

In short: Housey doesn't collect, store or share any personal information. There are no accounts, ads, analytics or tracking. Your settings stay on your own device.

This policy covers the Housey game at housey.webappslab.com and the Housey apps for Android and iOS. They're made by WebAppsLab ("we").

What we collect

Nothing. We don't run servers that receive data from the game, and we don't use analytics, advertising, crash reporting or any other third-party tracking tools. We never sell or share data, because we never have any.

What stays on your device

To remember your choices between games, Housey saves these settings on your device:

  • the name you type on the setup card (optional; it only labels you at the table)
  • the number of tickets and players, the calling speed, and whether the caller's voice and auto-dab are on
  • whether sound is on

On the website these are kept in your browser's local storage. In the apps they're kept in the app's private storage. They never leave your device, and we can't see them.

The website also saves a copy of its own files in your browser (a "service worker" cache) so the game opens without an internet connection. That copy holds only the game's files, not information about you.

The website

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub may record technical details of each visit, such as your IP address and browser type, to deliver the site and keep it secure. We don't receive or use those records. See GitHub's privacy statement for details.

The website uses no cookies. Its fonts and sounds come with the site, so it doesn't load anything from other companies.

The apps

  • No internet access. The Android app doesn't ask for internet permission, and neither app connects to the internet.
  • Caller's voice. The numbers are read out by your device's built-in text-to-speech engine, provided by your phone's maker, Google or Apple. Housey only gives it the words to say, such as "Two little ducks, 22". How the engine works is covered by its provider's privacy policy. You can turn the voice off on the setup card.
  • Vibration and screen. The app vibrates when you dab or win, and keeps the screen on during a game. Neither involves any personal data.
  • No sensitive permissions. The apps don't use your camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or files.

Deleting your data

Since everything is on your device, you're in control:

  • Website: clear this site's data in your browser settings.
  • Apps: uninstall the app, or clear its storage in your phone's settings.

Children

Housey is a family game suitable for all ages. Because it collects no personal information from anyone, it collects none from children either.

Changes to this policy

If we change how Housey handles information, we'll update this page and the date at the top before the change takes effect.
