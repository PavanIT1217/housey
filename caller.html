A Tambola number caller for your own game

Play Housey with real paper tickets: print tickets, hand them out, and let this page draw the numbers and read them out. It works on a phone, or on a laptop plugged into a TV for a big room.

  • Press Start calling and numbers come out on their own at the speed you set. Pause any time.
  • Prefer to go at your own pace? Tap the bag or Next number for each call.
  • Called a number by mistake? Undo puts it back in the bag.
  • The game is saved as you go, so refreshing the page or closing the browser doesn't lose it.

Keyboard: Space starts or pauses, N draws the next number, U undoes.

Questions

Do I need Housey tickets to use the number caller?

No. The caller works with any Tambola or Housie tickets. Housey tickets add a code and QR code, so you can check a winning ticket in a second.

Can I change how fast numbers are called?

Yes. Pick Relaxed, Normal or Quick, or set any time from 2 to 20 seconds between numbers. You can also tap for each number yourself.

Does the caller say the traditional Tambola calls?

Only if you want it to. By default it just says the number. Switch to Traditional to hear calls like "Two little ducks, 22".

What happens if I refresh the page?

Nothing is lost. The numbers called so far are saved in your browser, so you can carry on where you left off.
