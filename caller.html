A Tambola number caller for your own game

Play Housey with real paper tickets: print tickets, hand them out, and let this page draw the numbers and read them out. It works on a phone, or on a laptop plugged into a TV for a big room.

  • Press Start calling and numbers come out on their own at the speed you set. Pause any time.
  • Prefer to go at your own pace? Tap the bag or Next number for each call.
  • Called a number by mistake? Undo puts it back in the bag.
  • The game is saved as you go, so refreshing the page or closing the browser doesn't lose it.

Keyboard: Space starts or pauses, N draws the next number, U undoes.
