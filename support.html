How to play

  • Choose how many tickets you want, how many players sit at the table and how fast the caller goes. Choose I'll tap to draw each coin yourself by tapping the bag.
  • Numbers 1–90 come out of the bag one at a time. When a called number is on your ticket, tap it to dab it with ink. Tap again to undo.
  • Complete a prize and press its button to claim it before the other players do:
    • Early Five: the first five numbers dabbed on a ticket
    • Top, Middle or Bottom Line: every number in that row
    • Full House: all 15 numbers on a ticket
  • Claim a prize you haven't got and the caller shouts "Bogey!". The game ends when someone wins Full House.

Common questions

I can't hear the caller

Check that sound is on (the speaker button at the top), that "Caller's voice" is ticked on the setup card, and that your phone isn't on silent. The apps use your phone's text-to-speech voice. If none is installed, turn on text-to-speech in your phone's accessibility or language settings. On the website, some browsers only play sound after you've tapped the page once.

It says a number "hasn't been called yet"

You dabbed a number that hasn't come out of the bag. Tap it again to remove the ink. If it gets called later, the warning goes away by itself.

I finished a line but the claim didn't work

Every number in the prize has to be both called and dabbed. If you see "So close!", look for a called number you haven't dabbed yet, or turn on "Auto-dab for me" on the setup card.

Does it work offline?

Yes. The apps never need the internet. The website saves itself on your device after your first visit and then works without a connection.

Can I play with friends on their own phones?

Not yet. Each game is you against computer players on one device. For a party, put the game on a big screen and let everyone call out their claims.

Does Housey cost anything or involve real money?

No. Housey is free, with no ads or in-app purchases, and prizes are just points.

How do I reset my settings?

On the website, clear this site's data in your browser. In the apps, clear the app's storage in your phone's settings, or reinstall it. See the Privacy Policy for what is stored.
