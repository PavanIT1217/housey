Printable Tambola tickets, free

Hosting Tambola at home, a kitty party or an office Diwali event? Make every player a ticket here, print them (or save a PDF to share), and run the game with the number caller. Each ticket follows the usual Housie rules: 3 rows and 9 columns, 15 numbers, 5 in every row.

Are the printable Tambola tickets free?

Yes. Make and print as many tickets as you like, with no sign-up and no watermark.

Are all the tickets different?

Yes. Every ticket gets its own code, and the numbers come from the code, so no two tickets in a batch are the same.

What is the QR code on each ticket for?

Scanning it opens the ticket on a phone, where it can be dabbed on screen. The number caller can also check any ticket by its code when someone claims a prize.

Can I print tickets in colour or black and white?

Both. Choose one of seven colours, mixed colours for a party, or the black ink saver.
